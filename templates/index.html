Job Listing Scorer

Powered by Jev (TypeSafe AI). Scores listings for fit, urgency, and red flags — it never applies or submits anything for you.

e.g. "Backend engineer, 4 years Node.js/TypeScript/Postgres, looking for remote roles at seed-to-series-B startups" — or upload your resume and it fills this in from your actual role, skills, and experience.
This text is sent to TypeSafe (Jev) for every listing scored, and to Google (Gemini) for resume comparisons and cover letters — both external APIs. Email and phone number are automatically redacted before anything is sent; nothing else is.
Pulls from public, ToS-friendly sources. LinkedIn/Indeed aren't included — those don't offer a public feed. Only listings posted in the last 7 days are shown; older ones are dropped.
Optional: specific companies (comma-separated slugs)
